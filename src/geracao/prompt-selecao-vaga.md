## A résumé for one job

This résumé is aimed at the job posting in `<job>`: its role, what it asks for (the required items first, then the nice to have, each with the records that already cover it) and what the person never used (`not_used`).

- **Choose and order for the job.** The records that answer the required items first, then the nice to have, then the strongest of the rest to fill the page. Within a group, the records that answer the job go first; the person's importance decides between two that answer it equally.
- **A record about managing people stays out**, whatever the job asks.
- **The job's words, where the record carries them.** Name the practices and technologies the job asks for in the posting's own words, but only when the record (or, for the stack line, the Affiliation's stack) carries them. A keyword the Corpus does not carry stays out.
- **What the person never used stays out** (`not_used`): no bullet, stack line or summary claims it, not even as "exposure to".
- **The headline** (`header_role`): the job's role without its seniority word ("Senior Backend Engineer" → "Backend Engineer"), only when one of the person's Affiliations is that same role, in any language ("Desenvolvedor Back-end" is a "Backend Engineer"). Otherwise the role the person held, in English, with no seniority; or null. A code check refuses a headline no Affiliation holds up.
- **The summary** speaks to the job's main asks that the records support, in one line. What the Corpus lacks stays absent: never gesture at a requirement no record carries.
- **A requirement nothing covers is fine.** The page tells what the person did; it is never stretched to cover the job.
