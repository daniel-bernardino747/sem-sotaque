## The job

This résumé is aimed at the job in `<job>`. You get the job only to judge the aim of two lines, in a fourth pass:

4. **The aim** (passe `mira`), on `[header_role]` and `[summary]` only: does the headline name the job's role (with no seniority) or the role the person plainly held; does the summary speak to what the job asks most, without claiming what the page does not show? The aim is kept as a note for the person, never rewritten: so never suggest adding to the summary what no line of the page shows.

A requirement of the job that no line covers is **not a problem of the page**: the person may not have it, and a résumé never invents it. If you report one, use passe `requisito_ausente`; it is set aside and never sent to be rewritten.
