## The job

This résumé is aimed at the job in `<job>` (its role, and what the person never used). Only the headline and the summary aim at it:

- `header_role`: the job's role without its seniority word, only when an Affiliation of the Corpus is that same role (in any language); otherwise the role the person held, in English, with no seniority; or `remover` it.
- The summary speaks to the job's main asks that the records support.
- What the person never used (`not_used`) and what no record carries stay out of every line. A requirement no line covers is fine: it is never a reason to add or stretch a line.
